09HOLNESS Dollar

The Economic Language of the HOLNESS Ecosystem

Most loyalty systems reward spending.

HOLNESS seeks to reward participation in human wellbeing.

The HOLNESS Dollar is designed as a shared value layer connecting members, businesses and communities throughout the ecosystem.

As individuals engage with participating organisations, healthy choices and ecosystem participation can create additional value and rewards.

Benefits For Individuals

  • Rewards for participation
  • Greater ecosystem engagement
  • Access to benefits and experiences
  • Encouragement of positive lifestyle choices

Benefits For Partners

  • Greater customer retention
  • Increased repeat engagement
  • Referral opportunities
  • Stronger customer relationships
  • Enhanced lifetime value

Benefits For the Ecosystem

  • Increased collaboration
  • Stronger network effects
  • Greater participation
  • Sustainable ecosystem growth
