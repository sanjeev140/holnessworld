HOLNESS Public Good Award™

The Global Recognition For Human Flourishing

The HOLNESS Public Good Award is the highest recognition within the HOLNESS ecosystem. It recognises organisations that create measurable improvements in human wellbeing, opportunity, and societal prosperity.

The HOLNESS Public Good Award is not a marketing accolade. It is an independent recognition of measurable Human Capital creation and societal contribution.

Its purpose is to become the global benchmark for organisations committed to Human Flourishing.

Why It Matters

The HOLNESS Public Good Award fills this gap.

Most organisations receive recognition for:

  • Revenue growth
  • Market performance
  • Innovation
  • Sustainability initiatives

Few receive recognition for:

  • Human flourishing created
  • Community wellbeing improved
  • Shared value generated
  • Human capital developed
What It Recognises

The award evaluates measurable outcomes such as:

The emphasis is on outcomes rather than activities.

Health improvementEducational advancementFinancial resilienceWomen's empowermentCommunity wellbeingEnvironmental engagementHuman flourishing

  • Societal credibility
